GST Calculator India

What This Calculator Estimates

Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India is a multi-tier consumption tax administered jointly by the central and state governments, with different rate slabs applying to different categories of goods and services rather than a single uniform rate. Because GST in India uses multiple slabs (unlike a flat-rate VAT system), this calculator lets you enter the specific rate applicable to your product or service category to estimate the GST amount and total price, rather than assuming one fixed percentage applies universally.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Confirm the exact GST slab for your specific product or service via its HSN or SAC code.
  • Understand whether your transaction is intra-state (CGST+SGST) or inter-state (IGST) before filing.
  • Check current registration thresholds, which can vary by state and business category.
  • Keep input tax credit records if you're a registered business claiming credits.
  • Revisit rate assumptions whenever the GST Council announces slab changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does India have a single GST rate or multiple slabs?

India uses multiple GST slabs applying to different categories of goods and services, unlike countries with a single flat VAT/GST rate — you need to know which slab applies to your specific item.

How do I know which GST rate applies to my product?

The GST Council publishes rate schedules by HSN (Harmonized System of Nomenclature) code for goods and SAC (Services Accounting Code) for services — check current GST rate notifications for your specific category.

Is GST the same as CGST and SGST combined?

For intra-state transactions, GST is typically split into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST), which together equal the total GST rate; inter-state transactions use Integrated GST (IGST) instead.

Who is required to register for GST in India?

Businesses exceeding the GST registration threshold (which varies by state and business type) must register; certain categories must register regardless of turnover.

How is the GST-inclusive total calculated?

This calculator multiplies your amount by the GST rate you enter to estimate the tax, then adds that to your original amount for the GST-inclusive total.
