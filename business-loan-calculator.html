Frequently Asked Questions

What factors affect business loan interest rates?

Business credit history, time in business, revenue, industry risk, collateral, and loan type all typically affect the rate a lender offers, with newer or higher-risk businesses often facing higher rates.

What's the difference between a term loan and a line of credit?

A term loan provides a lump sum repaid on a fixed schedule, while a line of credit offers flexible access to funds up to a limit, with interest charged only on the amount drawn — the right choice depends on your specific cash flow needs.

Are SBA loans different from standard business loans?

SBA-backed loans often offer more favorable rates and terms since the government guarantees a portion of the loan, but they typically involve more paperwork and a longer approval process than a standard business loan.

Does a longer loan term always mean paying more?

A longer term lowers monthly payments but generally increases total interest paid over the life of the loan, so the right trade-off depends on your business's cash flow needs versus total cost tolerance.

Should I secure a business loan with collateral?

Secured loans (backed by business or personal assets) often come with better rates than unsecured options, but they also put those assets at risk if you can't repay — weigh this trade-off carefully.
