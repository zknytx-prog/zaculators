Frequently Asked Questions

What deductions typically reduce gross salary in Nigeria?

Personal Income Tax (after Consolidated Relief Allowance), pension contributions, and sometimes National Housing Fund (NHF) contributions are common deductions from gross salary.

Is pension contribution mandatory in Nigeria?

Yes, under the Contributory Pension Scheme, both employer and employee are generally required to contribute a percentage of salary for employees covered under the Pension Reform Act.

Does this calculator compute exact income tax automatically?

No, pair this with the dedicated Nigeria Income Tax Calculator for a more precise estimate based on current PITA bands and the Consolidated Relief Allowance.

How does state of residence affect Nigerian salary tax?

Personal Income Tax is generally administered by the State Internal Revenue Service of your state of residence, though rates and bands follow the federal PITA framework.

What is the Consolidated Relief Allowance?

It's a statutory relief subtracted from gross income before tax bands are applied, reducing the taxable base — check current PITA provisions for the exact calculation.
