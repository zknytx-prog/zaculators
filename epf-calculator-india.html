What This Calculator Estimates

The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) in India is a mandatory retirement savings scheme administered by the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), requiring contributions from both employer and employee, generally calculated as a percentage of basic salary plus dearness allowance, subject to a statutory wage ceiling for mandatory contributions. Because contribution rates, wage ceilings, and interest rates are reviewed periodically, this calculator uses an editable rate so your estimate reflects current applicable figures.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Use basic salary plus dearness allowance, not full gross salary, for an accurate base.
  • Check the current statutory wage ceiling for mandatory contributions.
  • Remember part of the employer's contribution is typically redirected to the EPS pension scheme.
  • Check EPFO's current declared interest rate separately from the contribution percentage.
  • Keep your UAN and passbook handy to cross-check actual credited amounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

What salary is used for EPF contribution calculation?

Generally basic salary plus dearness allowance is used as the base, not the full gross salary including all allowances.

Is there a wage ceiling for mandatory EPF contributions?

Yes, EPFO applies a statutory wage ceiling above which contributions become optional at the employer's discretion — check EPFO's current published ceiling.

Do employer and employee contribute the same percentage?

Both typically contribute at similar headline rates, though a portion of the employer's contribution is usually redirected to the Employees' Pension Scheme (EPS) rather than the EPF account itself.

How is EPF interest calculated?

EPFO declares an annual interest rate credited to member accounts, which is separate from the contribution percentage — check EPFO's current declared rate for accurate interest estimates.

Can I withdraw my EPF balance before retirement?

Partial withdrawals are permitted under specific circumstances (such as housing, medical emergencies, or unemployment) as per EPFO rules — confirm current withdrawal conditions with EPFO.
