What This Calculator Estimates

A Fixed Deposit (FD) in India is a low-risk investment where you deposit a lump sum with a bank or NBFC for a fixed term at a predetermined interest rate, with returns generally more predictable than market-linked investments but typically lower than potential equity returns over the long run. This calculator estimates maturity value using your deposit amount, interest rate, and tenure, accounting for compounding as typically applied by Indian banks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is FD interest typically compounded in India?

Most Indian banks compound FD interest quarterly, though this can vary by bank and specific product — check your bank's terms for the exact compounding frequency used.

Are FD returns guaranteed?

FD returns are generally considered low-risk and predictable since the rate is fixed at the time of deposit, though returns are still subject to the bank's creditworthiness and are typically lower than potential market-linked investment returns.

Is FD interest taxable in India?

Yes, FD interest is generally taxable as per your income tax slab, and banks may deduct TDS if interest income exceeds a certain threshold — check current Income Tax Department rules for specifics.

Can I withdraw an FD before maturity?

Most banks allow premature withdrawal, though this typically comes with a penalty (reduced interest rate) — check your specific bank's terms before committing to a long tenure.

How does FD compare to other investment options in India?

FDs generally offer more predictable, lower returns compared to market-linked options like mutual funds or SIPs, making them popular for capital preservation and short-to-medium-term goals rather than long-term wealth building.
