VAT Calculator South Africa

What This Calculator Estimates

Value Added Tax in South Africa is administered by SARS and applied at a flat statutory rate on most goods and services, with certain items zero-rated (such as some basic foodstuffs) to reduce the tax burden on essential goods. Because VAT is a single flat percentage, this calculator multiplies your entered amount by the rate you provide — keep it current with SARS's published rate, especially since South Africa has adjusted its VAT rate in recent years.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Confirm the current VAT rate with SARS before finalizing invoices or filings.
  • Check whether your specific goods fall under the zero-rated basic foodstuffs list.
  • Keep VAT collected and VAT paid records separate for accurate return filing.
  • Register for VAT proactively if you expect to cross the mandatory threshold soon.
  • Recheck the rate periodically, especially around Budget Speech announcements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current VAT rate in South Africa?

SARS publishes South Africa's current VAT rate on its official website — confirm this before relying on it for invoicing, since the rate has been adjusted in recent years.

Are all goods subject to VAT in South Africa?

No, certain basic foodstuffs and other specified items are zero-rated to reduce the cost burden on essentials — check SARS's current zero-rated list.

Who is required to register for VAT in South Africa?

Businesses with taxable supplies exceeding SARS's mandatory registration threshold must register; voluntary registration is available below that threshold.

How is VAT-inclusive price calculated?

This calculator multiplies your amount by the VAT rate to estimate the tax, then adds that to your original amount for the VAT-inclusive total.

How often does South Africa change its VAT rate?

VAT rate changes are relatively infrequent but have occurred, often announced during the annual Budget Speech — check SARS for the current rate.
