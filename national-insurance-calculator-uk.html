Frequently Asked Questions

Is National Insurance the same as Income Tax?

No, they're separate systems — Income Tax funds general government spending while National Insurance specifically funds state benefits and pension entitlement, using its own thresholds.

Do employees and self-employed people pay the same National Insurance?

No, employees typically pay Class 1 National Insurance, while the self-employed pay Class 2 and/or Class 4 depending on profit levels — rates and thresholds differ between these classes.

Does National Insurance affect my State Pension?

Yes, your National Insurance contribution record (measured in qualifying years) determines your eligibility for and amount of the UK State Pension.

Does this calculator apply the correct NI class automatically?

No, enter the rate applicable to your specific NI class (employee, employer, or self-employed) for an accurate estimate.

Where can I confirm current official NI rates and thresholds?

HMRC publishes current National Insurance thresholds and rates by class on its official website.

This calculator provides UK National Insurance estimates only. Official thresholds, classes, and rates can change, so verify current rules before relying on the result.
