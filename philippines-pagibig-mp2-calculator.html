Philippines Pag-IBIG MP2 Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Pag-IBIG MP2 (Modified Pag-IBIG II) is a voluntary savings program in the Philippines offered by the Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund), allowing members to make additional voluntary contributions beyond the mandatory Pag-IBIG contribution, typically earning a higher dividend rate than the regular savings program with a five-year maturity term. Because dividend rates are declared annually and are not fixed in advance, this calculator uses an editable rate so you can estimate returns using the most recently declared rate or a rate you want to model.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between regular Pag-IBIG savings and MP2?

The regular Pag-IBIG contribution is mandatory for covered employees, while MP2 is a voluntary additional savings program offering a typically higher dividend rate with a five-year lock-in term.

Are MP2 dividend rates fixed?

No, Pag-IBIG Fund declares dividend rates annually based on fund performance, so actual returns can vary from year to year and from what you estimate here.

Can I withdraw MP2 savings before five years?

MP2 is designed as a five-year term savings product; early withdrawal terms and any dividend forfeiture should be confirmed directly with Pag-IBIG Fund.

Who can open an MP2 account?

Both current Pag-IBIG members (employed, self-employed, or voluntary) and even non-members in some cases can open an MP2 account — check Pag-IBIG Fund's current eligibility rules.

Where can I check the current declared dividend rate?

Pag-IBIG Fund announces dividend rates annually on its official website and through official announcements — use that figure for the most accurate estimate.
