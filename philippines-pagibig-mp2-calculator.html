Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between regular Pag-IBIG savings and MP2?

MP2 is a voluntary enhancement to the standard mandatory Pag-IBIG fund, typically offering higher dividend rates in exchange for a longer 5-year lock-in period, making it attractive for long-term savers seeking better returns.

How are MP2 dividends typically calculated?

Dividends are generally calculated based on your total contributions and the fund's declared annual dividend rate, which is announced periodically and can vary from year to year based on the fund's performance.

Is the MP2 dividend rate guaranteed?

No, the dividend rate is declared periodically by Pag-IBIG based on fund performance and is not a guaranteed fixed rate, though it has often historically been competitive with other savings options in the Philippines.

What happens if I withdraw MP2 savings before the 5-year maturity?

MP2 is designed as a 5-year term savings product, and early withdrawal terms or penalties (if any) should be confirmed with current official Pag-IBIG Fund guidance before committing funds you may need earlier.

Should I verify current dividend rates before relying on this estimate?

Yes, dividend rates are declared periodically and can change, so verify the current official rate from Pag-IBIG Fund before relying on this estimate for long-term financial planning.
