Salary Calculator India

What This Calculator Estimates

Indian salaries typically follow a Cost to Company (CTC) structure that includes basic salary, allowances like HRA, and employer contributions to EPF, with take-home pay reduced by income tax (TDS), employee EPF contribution, and professional tax where applicable. Because CTC often overstates what actually reaches your bank account, this calculator helps model the gap between CTC and net salary — pair it with the Income Tax, EPF, and HRA calculators for a fuller picture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is CTC different from take-home salary in India?

CTC includes employer contributions (like EPF and insurance) and sometimes non-cash benefits that never reach your bank account, so take-home pay is meaningfully lower than the headline CTC figure.

What deductions typically reduce gross salary to net salary?

Income tax (TDS), employee EPF contribution, professional tax (in applicable states), and sometimes other benefit deductions reduce gross salary to net take-home pay.

Does this calculator compute exact TDS automatically?

No, pair this with the Income Tax Calculator India for a more precise estimate based on current tax slabs and your specific deductions and exemptions.

What is professional tax and does it apply everywhere in India?

Professional tax is a state-level tax on employment income, and it only applies in certain states — check whether your state levies it and at what rate.

How does HRA exemption affect my net salary?

HRA exemption reduces your taxable income if you pay rent, which can lower your TDS and increase your effective take-home pay — use the HRA Calculator India to estimate this separately.
