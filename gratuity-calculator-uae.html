Gratuity Calculator UAE

What This Calculator Estimates

End-of-service gratuity in the UAE is a statutory benefit for employees under the UAE Labour Law, calculated based on basic salary and total years of continuous service, with the formula differing depending on whether the employee resigns or is terminated, and often prorated differently for the first five years versus service beyond that. Because eligibility and calculation details can vary by contract type and length of service, this calculator provides a general estimate that you should verify against your specific employment terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UAE gratuity based on basic salary or total salary?

Gratuity is calculated based on basic salary only, excluding allowances such as housing, transport, or other benefits.

Does resigning affect the gratuity amount compared to being terminated?

Historically, UAE labour law distinguished between resignation and termination for gratuity calculation purposes, though rules have evolved — check the current Labour Law provisions or a legal advisor for your specific situation.

Is there a minimum service period required for gratuity?

Generally, at least one year of continuous service is required to qualify for any end-of-service gratuity under UAE law.

How is gratuity calculated for service beyond five years?

The formula typically changes for years of service beyond the initial threshold, often calculated at a higher number of days' pay per year — verify the exact current formula with official UAE Labour Law resources.

Are unpaid leave periods counted toward gratuity service?

Extended unpaid leave periods may not count toward continuous service for gratuity purposes, depending on the circumstances — confirm with your HR department or legal counsel.
