Frequently Asked Questions

What's the conversion factor between miles and kilometers?

One mile equals approximately 1.60934 kilometers, a fixed conversion factor used to convert between these two common distance units.

How do I convert feet to meters?

One foot equals approximately 0.3048 meters — multiply your feet value by this factor to get the equivalent in meters.

Why do the US and most other countries use different distance units?

The United States primarily uses imperial units (miles, feet, inches) for everyday measurements, while most other countries have adopted the metric system (kilometers, meters) as their standard.

Is metric or imperial more precise for measurement?

Neither system is inherently more precise — both can express measurements to any level of precision needed; the difference is in the base units and conversion factors used, not underlying accuracy.

Where is distance conversion commonly needed?

Common uses include international travel planning, comparing running or cycling distances, cooking/DIY projects using different unit systems, and general reference when working across different measurement standards.
