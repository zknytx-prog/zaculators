What This Calculator Estimates

Recommended exercise duration depends on your specific fitness goals, current fitness level, and the type of activity, with general population health guidelines suggesting a baseline amount of moderate-to-vigorous activity per week for general health benefits, while performance or weight-management goals may call for more. This calculator helps estimate how exercise duration adds up toward weekly targets based on the sessions and durations you enter.

Formula / Method Used

The calculator estimates calories burned per minute with the common MET-based formula: MET x 3.5 x body weight in kg / 200. It then divides your calorie target by that per-minute estimate to calculate workout duration in minutes.

Worked Example

If your goal is to burn 400 calories, you weigh 70 kg, and you select running with a MET value of 8, the calculator estimates calories burned per minute and then calculates the number of minutes needed to reach the 400-calorie goal.

What the Result Means

The result is a planning estimate, not a guarantee. It can help you compare activities or set a workout target, but real calorie burn varies with pace, fitness level, movement efficiency, and how closely your session matches the chosen intensity.

Common Mistakes

  1. Choosing an activity intensity that does not match your real workout pace.
  2. Assuming machine calorie numbers and formula estimates will match exactly.
  3. Using a stale body-weight estimate after significant change.
  4. Treating calorie burn as the only meaningful workout goal.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Aim for a mix of moderate and vigorous activity across the week for general health benefits.
  • Don't hesitate to break up exercise into shorter sessions if that fits your schedule better.
  • Include strength training alongside cardio activity for balanced fitness benefits.
  • Track your weekly total to see how individual sessions add up toward broader goals.
  • Consult a fitness professional for a plan tailored to specific performance or weight goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much exercise is generally recommended per week?

General health guidelines commonly suggest a baseline amount of moderate-to-vigorous aerobic activity per week for general health benefits, plus strength training on multiple days, though specific goals may call for more.

Does exercise duration matter more than intensity?

Both matter, and they can complement each other — shorter high-intensity sessions and longer moderate-intensity sessions can both contribute meaningfully to weekly activity targets, depending on your goals and preferences.

Can I break up exercise into shorter sessions throughout the day?

Yes, accumulating activity in shorter bouts throughout the day generally counts toward weekly totals and can be just as beneficial as one continuous session for many health outcomes.

Does this calculator account for exercise type?

It totals up duration across sessions you enter; the specific health benefit also depends on activity type and intensity, which vary by exercise.

How do I know if I'm exercising enough for my specific goals?

General health guidelines provide a starting baseline, but specific goals (weight loss, athletic performance, etc.) often benefit from a more tailored plan — consider consulting a fitness professional for personalized guidance.

General Disclaimer

This calculator is for general fitness planning only. It is not medical advice, and actual calorie burn can differ from estimates because of intensity, conditioning, biomechanics, and device measurement differences.
