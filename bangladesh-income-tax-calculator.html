Bangladesh Income Tax Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Bangladeshi personal income tax is administered by the National Board of Revenue (NBR) using a progressive slab system, with a tax-free threshold followed by increasing rates across higher income bands. Because NBR revises slabs and the tax-free threshold through the annual Finance Act, and thresholds can differ for certain categories (such as women, senior citizens, or persons with disabilities), this calculator uses an editable rate so your estimate can reflect current guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who administers income tax in Bangladesh?

The National Board of Revenue (NBR) sets and enforces income tax rules, including the annual slab structure announced through the Finance Act.

Is there a tax-free threshold in Bangladesh?

Yes, NBR sets a tax-free income threshold below which no tax is owed, with this threshold sometimes varying for specific categories like women, senior citizens, and persons with disabilities.

How often are tax slabs updated in Bangladesh?

Slabs are typically revised annually through the Finance Act, usually alongside the national budget announcement.

Does this calculator apply the tax-free threshold automatically?

No, subtract your applicable tax-free threshold from your income before entering it, or adjust the rate to reflect your effective burden.

Where can I find current official tax slabs?

The National Board of Revenue (NBR) publishes current slab tables on its official website following each budget cycle.
