Frequently Asked Questions

What do HOA fees typically cover?

Common coverage includes maintenance of shared spaces and amenities, building insurance (for condos), landscaping, and contributions to a reserve fund for future major repairs or replacements.

Why do HOA fees vary so much between communities?

Fees depend heavily on the amenities offered (pools, gyms, security, concierge services), the age and condition of shared infrastructure, and how well-funded the community's reserve fund is.

Can HOA fees increase over time?

Yes, HOA fees commonly increase to keep pace with rising maintenance costs, insurance premiums, or to fund reserve accounts for anticipated major repairs, so budgeting some room for increases is wise.

What happens if I don't pay HOA fees?

Consequences vary by association and jurisdiction but can include late fees, liens on the property, and in serious cases, foreclosure proceedings — HOA fees are a legally binding obligation, not optional.

Should HOA fees factor into a home affordability decision?

Yes, HOA fees function like an additional recurring housing cost similar to property tax or insurance, and should be included when calculating total monthly housing costs for affordability purposes.
