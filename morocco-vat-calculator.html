Frequently Asked Questions

What is Morocco's standard VAT rate?

Morocco applies a standard VAT rate to most goods and services, with several reduced rates applying to specific sectors and product categories — check current official rates from the Moroccan tax authority as these can be updated.

What categories qualify for reduced VAT rates in Morocco?

Morocco's VAT system typically applies reduced rates to certain categories such as specific food products, tourism-related services, and other designated sectors, with the specific list set by tax legislation.

Is VAT included in displayed retail prices in Morocco?

Retail pricing conventions can vary, so check whether displayed prices are VAT-inclusive or exclusive for your specific purchase or business context in Morocco.

Do businesses need to register for VAT in Morocco?

Generally, businesses exceeding certain revenue thresholds are required to register for VAT and charge it on applicable sales — specific registration thresholds and requirements should be confirmed with current Moroccan tax authority guidance.

Should I verify current VAT rates before relying on this calculator?

Yes, VAT rates and category classifications can be adjusted through Moroccan tax policy changes, so verify current official rates before relying on this estimate for business or purchase planning.
