Frequently Asked Questions

What's the conversion factor between pounds and kilograms?

One pound equals approximately 0.453592 kilograms, a fixed conversion factor used to convert between these two common weight units.

How do I convert ounces to grams?

One ounce equals approximately 28.3495 grams — multiply your ounce value by this factor to get the equivalent in grams.

Why do the US and most other countries use different weight units?

The United States primarily uses imperial units (pounds, ounces) for everyday measurements, while most other countries have adopted the metric system (kilograms, grams) as their standard.

Is weight the same as mass in these conversions?

In everyday usage, weight and mass are often used interchangeably, though technically weight refers to the force of gravity on mass — for practical conversion purposes, this distinction rarely matters.

Where is weight conversion commonly needed?

Common uses include cooking with recipes from different countries, tracking body weight or fitness goals, shipping and postal calculations, and general reference across different measurement standards.
