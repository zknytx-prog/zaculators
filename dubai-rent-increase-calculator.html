Dubai Rent Increase Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Rent increases in Dubai are regulated by the Dubai Land Department (DLD) using the RERA Rental Index, which caps how much a landlord can increase rent based on how far the current rent is below the market average for similar properties in the same area — properties already at or near market rate may see no permitted increase, while significantly under-market rents can see larger caps. This calculator provides a general estimate — always verify the exact permitted percentage against the current official RERA index for your specific area and property type.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can landlords in Dubai increase rent by any amount they choose?

No, rent increases are capped by the RERA Rental Index rules, which base the permitted increase on how far your current rent sits below the market average for comparable properties in your area.

What is the RERA Rental Index?

It's an official index published by the Dubai Land Department comparing average rents for different property types and locations, used to determine the maximum legally permitted rent increase for renewing tenants.

Does a rent already at market rate face any increase cap?

If your current rent is already at or above the RERA index average for comparable properties, landlords generally cannot increase rent at renewal, or increases are capped at a lower percentage.

Does this calculator apply the exact current RERA percentage bands automatically?

No, this calculator provides a general estimate — check the Dubai Land Department's official RERA calculator for the precise legally permitted increase for your specific property and area.

Where can I verify the official rent increase percentage for my property?

The Dubai Land Department (DLD) provides an official RERA Rental Increase Calculator on its website that uses current index data for your specific area and property type.
