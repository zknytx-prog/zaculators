Dubai Rent Increase Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Dubai regulates permissible annual rent increases through the RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Agency) rental index, which caps increase percentages based on how far current rent sits below the average market rate for similar properties in the area. This calculator estimates rent increase amounts using the current rent and increase percentage you provide, useful for tenants and landlords navigating Dubai's rent regulation framework.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Dubai's RERA rental index affect allowable rent increases?

The RERA index compares your current rent to the average market rate for similar properties in your area — the further below market rate your current rent is, the higher the percentage increase a landlord may be permitted to apply.

Is there a cap on rent increases if current rent matches market rate?

Generally, if current rent is already at or near the average market rate according to the RERA index, landlords may not be permitted any increase, or only a minimal one, under Dubai's rental regulations.

How can tenants check the RERA rental index for their area?

The Dubai Land Department provides an online RERA rental index calculator/index tool where tenants and landlords can check average rates for specific areas and property types to verify a proposed increase is compliant.

What should a tenant do if they believe a rent increase exceeds legal limits?

Tenants who believe a proposed increase exceeds RERA-permitted limits can typically raise a dispute with the Rental Disputes Settlement Centre in Dubai, which has authority over such rental disagreements.

Does this calculator reflect official RERA index figures?

No, verify actual permissible increase percentages against the official Dubai Land Department RERA index for your specific area and property type before relying on this estimate.
