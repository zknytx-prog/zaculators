Frequently Asked Questions

Is Land Transfer Tax the same across all Canadian provinces?

No, each province sets its own Land Transfer Tax rates and rebate rules, and some provinces (like Alberta) have no land transfer tax at all, using a smaller title registration fee instead.

Does Toronto have an additional land transfer tax?

Yes, the City of Toronto levies its own municipal Land Transfer Tax on top of Ontario's provincial tax, making Toronto purchases notably higher than elsewhere in Ontario.

Do first-time homebuyers get a rebate?

Many provinces offer a rebate or reduction for first-time homebuyers, though eligibility criteria and maximum rebate amounts vary — check your specific province's current rules.

Is Land Transfer Tax progressive?

In most provinces, yes — the tax is calculated across bands, so different portions of the purchase price are taxed at different marginal rates.

Where can I confirm current official rates?

Your provincial ministry of finance (and, in Toronto's case, the city's official municipal land transfer tax page) publishes current rate tables and rebate eligibility.
