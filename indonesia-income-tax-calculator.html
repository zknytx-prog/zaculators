Indonesia Income Tax Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Indonesian personal income tax (PPh 21 for employment income) is administered by the Direktorat Jenderal Pajak (DJP) using a progressive bracket system, with rates rising across income bands after the Penghasilan Tidak Kena Pajak (PTKP) non-taxable income threshold is applied. Because PTKP thresholds vary based on marital status and number of dependents, and bands are subject to legislative change, this calculator uses an editable rate so you can model your specific situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PTKP in Indonesian income tax?

PTKP (Penghasilan Tidak Kena Pajak) is the non-taxable income threshold, which varies based on marital status and number of dependents, and is subtracted from gross income before tax bands apply.

Is Indonesian income tax progressive or flat?

Progressive — income above the PTKP threshold is taxed across graduated bands, so higher earners face higher marginal rates.

Who withholds PPh 21 for employees?

Employers are generally responsible for calculating and withholding PPh 21 from employee salaries each pay period and remitting it to DJP.

Does this calculator apply PTKP automatically?

No, factor your applicable PTKP threshold into your income figure manually before entering it, or adjust the rate to reflect your effective tax burden.

Where can I find current official tax bands and PTKP figures?

The Direktorat Jenderal Pajak (DJP) publishes current tax brackets and PTKP thresholds on its official website.
