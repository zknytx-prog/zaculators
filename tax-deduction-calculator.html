Tax Deduction Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Tax deductions reduce your taxable income before tax rates are applied, which is different from a tax credit (which reduces your tax bill directly, dollar for dollar) — understanding this distinction helps clarify how much a given deduction actually saves you, since the savings depend on your marginal tax rate. This calculator estimates the tax savings from a deduction by multiplying the deduction amount by your marginal tax rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a tax deduction and a tax credit?

A deduction reduces your taxable income (so its value depends on your marginal tax rate), while a tax credit reduces your tax bill directly on a dollar-for-dollar basis, regardless of your tax bracket.

How much does a deduction actually save me?

The tax savings from a deduction equals the deduction amount multiplied by your marginal tax rate — so the same deduction saves a higher-bracket taxpayer more than a lower-bracket taxpayer.

Does this calculator know which deductions I'm eligible for?

No, this estimates the tax savings from a deduction amount you enter, based on the marginal rate you provide — check your country's specific rules for what deductions you qualify for.

Should I take the standard deduction or itemize?

This depends on whether your itemizable deductions exceed the standard deduction amount available in your jurisdiction — compare both to see which results in lower taxable income.

Where can I find my country's list of eligible deductions?

Your national tax authority publishes current eligible deduction categories and any applicable limits or phase-outs.
