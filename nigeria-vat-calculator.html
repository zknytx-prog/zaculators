Nigeria VAT Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Value Added Tax in Nigeria is a consumption tax applied to most goods and services at a flat statutory rate, collected by businesses on behalf of the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) at each stage of the supply chain. Because VAT is a single flat percentage rather than a bracketed system, this calculator simply multiplies your amount by the rate you enter — keep the rate editable so it always matches the current statutory rate rather than one that could change after legislative updates.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Confirm the current statutory VAT rate with FIRS before using this for invoicing or filing purposes.
  • Check whether your specific goods or services fall under VAT-exempt or zero-rated categories.
  • Registered businesses must remit collected VAT to FIRS on a regular schedule — this tool only estimates the amount, not the filing process.
  • Keep records of VAT collected and paid separately, since input VAT may be reclaimable depending on your business type.
  • Revisit the rate you use whenever FIRS or legislative updates change the statutory VAT percentage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current VAT rate in Nigeria?

Nigeria's VAT rate is set by statute and has changed via legislation before, so confirm the current rate with FIRS or a recent official source before using it for filing purposes.

Who is responsible for collecting VAT in Nigeria?

Businesses registered for VAT collect it from customers on taxable supplies and remit it to FIRS, typically on a monthly basis.

Are all goods and services subject to VAT?

No, certain goods and services are zero-rated or exempt under Nigerian VAT law. Check FIRS guidance for the current exemption list.

How does this calculator compute total price including VAT?

It multiplies your entered amount by the VAT rate to estimate the VAT amount, then adds that to your original amount for the VAT-inclusive total.

Is this the same as Nigeria's income tax?

No, VAT is a separate consumption tax charged on transactions, distinct from Nigeria's Personal Income Tax which is charged on individual earnings.

This calculator provides VAT estimates only. Official tax rates and treatment may vary, so verify current guidance before relying on the result.
