Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nigeria's standard VAT rate?

Nigeria applies a standard VAT rate to most taxable goods and services — check current official rates from the Federal Inland Revenue Service, as VAT rates can be adjusted through tax legislation.

What items are typically exempt or zero-rated for VAT in Nigeria?

Nigerian VAT law generally exempts or zero-rates certain categories including specific basic food items, medical and pharmaceutical products, and educational materials, though the specific list is set by legislation.

Do businesses need to register for VAT in Nigeria?

Businesses meeting certain criteria are generally required to register for VAT and charge it on applicable sales — specific registration thresholds and requirements should be confirmed with current Nigerian tax authority guidance.

Is VAT included in displayed prices in Nigeria?

Pricing display conventions can vary by business and sector, so confirm whether a specific price you're working with already includes VAT or whether it needs to be added separately.

Should I verify current VAT rates before relying on this calculator?

Yes, VAT rates and exemption categories can be adjusted through Nigerian tax policy changes, so verify current official rates before relying on this estimate for business or purchase planning.
