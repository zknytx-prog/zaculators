Frequently Asked Questions

What is South Korea's standard VAT rate?

South Korea applies a standard VAT rate to most taxable goods and services — check current official rates from the National Tax Service, as VAT rates can be adjusted through tax legislation.

What categories are typically exempt from VAT in South Korea?

Korean VAT law generally exempts certain categories including specific unprocessed agricultural products, medical and health services, and educational services, though the specific exemption list is set by legislation.

Do businesses need to register for VAT in South Korea?

Businesses conducting taxable transactions are generally required to register for VAT with Korean tax authorities and charge it on applicable sales — specific requirements should be confirmed with current official guidance.

Is VAT included in displayed retail prices in South Korea?

Retail pricing in South Korea is generally displayed VAT-inclusive for consumer purchases, though confirm this convention for your specific purchase or business context.

Should I verify current VAT rates before relying on this calculator?

Yes, VAT rates and exemption categories can be adjusted through Korean tax policy changes, so verify current official rates before relying on this estimate for business or purchase planning.
