Frequently Asked Questions

How is end-of-service gratuity typically calculated in Qatar?

Qatari labor law commonly calculates gratuity based on a set number of days' basic wage for each year of service, with the total accruing progressively over the employee's tenure with the employer.

Is basic salary or total compensation used for Qatar gratuity calculations?

Typically basic salary, excluding allowances like housing or transport, forms the calculation base under Qatari labor regulations, though specific contract terms should be confirmed with your employment agreement.

Does how employment ends affect gratuity entitlement in Qatar?

Yes, circumstances such as resignation versus termination, and whether termination was for cause, can affect gratuity calculation and entitlement under Qatari labor law.

Is there a minimum service period required to qualify for gratuity in Qatar?

Qatari labor law generally requires a minimum period of continuous service before gratuity entitlement applies — confirm the current specific threshold with official labor law guidance.

Should I verify this estimate against my employment contract and current law?

Yes, always verify this general estimate against your specific employment contract terms and current Qatari labor law before relying on the figure for financial planning.
