Frequently Asked Questions

How is overtime pay typically calculated?

Overtime pay is generally calculated by multiplying your regular hourly rate by a premium multiplier (commonly 1.5x, known as 'time and a half') for each hour worked beyond the standard threshold, then adding this to regular pay.

What is the standard overtime threshold?

This varies significantly by country and jurisdiction — many places use a 40-hour workweek threshold, though specific rules, exemptions, and premium rates differ, so check regulations applicable to your situation.

Are all employees eligible for overtime pay?

No, eligibility depends on employment classification, industry, and jurisdiction — some salaried or managerial positions are often exempt from overtime requirements under many labor law frameworks.

Can overtime premium rates be higher than 1.5x regular pay?

Yes, some jurisdictions or specific circumstances (like holiday work or hours beyond a certain daily threshold) can require even higher premium rates, such as double-time, depending on applicable regulations.

Does this calculator account for country-specific overtime regulations?

No, enter the specific threshold and premium rate applicable to your country and situation, since overtime rules vary significantly and this calculator provides a general estimation framework.

This calculator provides gross pay estimates only. Overtime rules, premiums, and deductions may vary by employer and location.
