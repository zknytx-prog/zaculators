Tanzania PAYE Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Tanzania's PAYE regime is overseen by the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) and applies progressive rates to employment income, meaning the tax rate increases across higher income bands rather than staying flat. Employers withhold the tax each pay period and remit it to TRA. Since TRA periodically adjusts the income bands and thresholds, this calculator keeps the rate editable so you can enter the figure that matches current guidance rather than a number baked in that could become outdated.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Confirm the current PAYE bands with TRA before using this for anything beyond a rough estimate.
  • Remember NSSF and other statutory contributions are calculated separately from PAYE.
  • If your income includes housing or transport allowances, check whether TRA treats them as taxable benefits.
  • Revisit this calculator whenever TRA announces new bands, typically tied to the national budget cycle.
  • Use your actual payslip as the final source of truth — this tool is for planning, not filing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who oversees PAYE in Tanzania?

The Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) administers PAYE, with employers responsible for monthly withholding and remittance.

Is Tanzania's PAYE the same for residents and non-residents?

No, non-resident employees are typically taxed differently than residents in Tanzania, so this simple calculator is best suited for a general resident-employee estimate.

Does this include NSSF or other statutory deductions?

No, this estimates PAYE only. Tanzanian payslips typically include additional statutory contributions separate from PAYE.

How often are TRA's PAYE bands updated?

TRA periodically reviews and updates income bands, often alongside the national budget cycle, so check TRA's official publications for the latest figures.

Can I use this to estimate annual tax from a monthly salary?

Yes — enter your monthly salary and the calculator will show both the monthly estimate and a straightforward annualized figure (monthly × 12).
