What This Calculator Estimates

Australian salaries are subject to progressive income tax administered by the ATO, along with the Medicare Levy and mandatory employer superannuation contributions on top of base salary. Because Australian job listings sometimes quote salary as inclusive or exclusive of superannuation, this calculator helps clarify the components — pair it with the Income Tax Calculator (or a dedicated Australian tax tool) for a precise take-home estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What deductions typically apply to an Australian salary?

Income tax (progressive ATO bands) and the Medicare Levy are the main deductions from take-home pay, while superannuation is typically paid on top of salary by the employer rather than deducted from it.

Is superannuation included in or separate from quoted salary?

This varies by employer — some quote salary packages inclusive of superannuation, others quote base salary with super paid additionally, so always clarify which applies to your offer.

What is the Medicare Levy?

It's an additional levy funding Australia's public healthcare system, generally calculated as a percentage of taxable income on top of standard income tax, with some exemptions and surcharges depending on income and private health cover.

Does this calculator compute exact income tax automatically?

No, this is for modeling salary components. Pair it with a dedicated Australian income tax calculator for a precise estimate based on current ATO tax brackets.

How much is the mandatory superannuation guarantee?

The ATO sets a minimum superannuation guarantee percentage that employers must contribute — check the current rate on the ATO's official website since it has increased over recent years.
