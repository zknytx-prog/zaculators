Ethiopia Income Tax Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

Ethiopia's personal income tax is administered by the Ministry of Revenue and applies progressive rates across income bands, meaning higher slices of income are taxed at increasingly higher marginal rates. Employers generally withhold tax from employment income each pay period. Since bands and rates can be revised through proclamations and directives, this calculator uses an editable rate so your estimate can track current guidance instead of a fixed figure that may become outdated.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Confirm current bands with the Ministry of Revenue, since directives can adjust thresholds outside of a fixed schedule.
  • Employment income and business income may follow different tax schedules — apply the correct one for your situation.
  • Check whether allowances or in-kind benefits you receive are treated as taxable under current rules.
  • Keep your payslip or business records handy to cross-check this estimate against actual withholding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who administers income tax in Ethiopia?

Ethiopia's Ministry of Revenue oversees personal income tax collection and enforcement, including employment income withholding.

Is Ethiopian income tax progressive or flat?

Progressive — the system uses graduated bands so the effective rate rises as income increases.

Does this apply to both salaried and business income?

The calculator works for any income figure you enter, but salaried employment income and business income can be taxed under different schedules in Ethiopia, so treat results for business income as a rough estimate only.

How current is the rate I should use?

Tax bands can be updated through government directives, so check with the Ministry of Revenue or a local tax advisor for the most current figures before filing.

Can this calculator handle annual income directly?

Yes, enter your income for whatever period (monthly or annual) you want to estimate, along with the appropriate rate for that bracket.
