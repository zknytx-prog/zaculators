Kuwait Indemnity Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

End-of-service indemnity in Kuwait is a statutory benefit under Kuwaiti Labour Law, generally calculated based on basic salary and years of continuous service, with different rates commonly applying to the first five years of service versus years beyond that, and the calculation can also vary depending on whether the employee resigns or is terminated. This calculator provides a general estimate — verify exact entitlements against your contract and current Kuwaiti Labour Law provisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What salary is used for Kuwait's indemnity calculation?

Generally the basic salary is used, excluding allowances such as housing or transport, though contract specifics can vary.

Does resignation reduce the indemnity payout in Kuwait?

Kuwaiti Labour Law has historically applied reduced entitlements for voluntary resignation, particularly for shorter service periods — check current provisions for exact rules.

Is there a minimum service period required?

Generally at least a set minimum period of continuous service is required for any entitlement, with full entitlement typically requiring longer tenure — confirm with official sources.

Does the rate change for service beyond five years?

Yes, the daily/monthly rate used in the calculation commonly changes for years of service beyond an initial threshold — verify current figures with official Labour Law provisions.

Should this be used for legal or HR disputes?

No, treat this as a planning estimate only. For disputes or exact entitlements, consult your employment contract, HR department, or a labour law professional.
