What This Calculator Estimates

Singapore's income tax system applies progressive rates to chargeable income after personal reliefs and deductions, with rates set by the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) and among the more moderate rate structures globally for many income levels. Frequently Asked Questions

How does Singapore's progressive tax rate system work?

Chargeable income (after reliefs and deductions) is divided into brackets, each taxed at its own progressively higher rate, meaning the overall effective tax rate is typically well below the top marginal bracket rate for most taxpayers.

What are common tax reliefs available under Singapore tax law?

Singapore offers various personal reliefs including earned income relief, spouse relief, child relief, and CPF contribution relief, among others — confirm current amounts and eligibility with official IRAS guidance.

Does tax residency status affect Singapore income tax treatment?

Yes, tax residency status significantly affects applicable rates and available reliefs under Singapore tax law, with residents generally taxed under progressive rates while non-residents may face different treatment.

How often does Singapore update tax brackets and reliefs?

Tax brackets, rates, and available reliefs are periodically reviewed and can be adjusted through the national budget, so using current-year figures is important for an accurate estimate.

Should I verify this estimate before filing with IRAS?

Yes, this provides a general estimate only — verify your actual tax liability with current official Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) guidance or a tax professional before filing.
