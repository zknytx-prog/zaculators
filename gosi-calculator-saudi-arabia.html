GOSI Calculator Saudi Arabia

What This Calculator Estimates

GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance) is Saudi Arabia's social insurance scheme, funded by mandatory contributions from both employer and employee on a percentage of salary, covering benefits like pensions, occupational hazards, and unemployment insurance (SANED) for Saudi nationals, with a narrower scope for non-Saudi employees. Contribution rates differ depending on nationality and whether the employee is covered under the full scheme or the reduced expatriate scheme. Because GOSI periodically adjusts rates and salary caps, this calculator keeps the percentage editable so your estimate can reflect the current applicable rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Saudi nationals and expatriates pay the same GOSI rate?

No. Saudi nationals are covered under a broader scheme including pensions and SANED unemployment insurance, while non-Saudi employees are typically covered only for occupational hazards, resulting in different contribution rates.

Is GOSI paid by the employee, employer, or both?

Both. Employers and employees each contribute a percentage of salary, with the employer's share typically higher for Saudi nationals due to the broader benefit coverage.

Is there a salary cap for GOSI contributions?

Yes, GOSI applies contribution ceilings on salary, meaning earnings above a certain threshold aren't subject to additional contributions. Check GOSI's official schedule for current caps.

How often does GOSI update its rates?

Rates and thresholds can be revised through GOSI regulations, so it's worth checking the official GOSI portal periodically, especially after any announced policy changes.

Does this calculator apply automatically to my nationality category?

No, you need to enter the rate applicable to your specific category (Saudi or non-Saudi) manually, since the calculator uses a single editable rate field.
