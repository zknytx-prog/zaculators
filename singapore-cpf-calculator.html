Frequently Asked Questions

How are CPF contributions split between employee and employer?

CPF requires contributions from both employee and employer, calculated as percentages of wages, with the combined amount allocated across the employee's Ordinary, Special, and MediSave accounts.

Do CPF contribution rates vary by age?

Yes, Singapore's CPF contribution rates typically decrease for older employees, reflecting adjusted rates as workers age, particularly noticeable for employees above certain age thresholds.

Does CPF apply to Permanent Residents the same way as citizens?

CPF contribution rates can differ for Singapore Permanent Residents, particularly in their first two years of PR status, compared to full Singapore citizens — confirm which schedule applies to your status.

What are the different CPF account types used for?

CPF contributions are typically allocated across the Ordinary Account (housing, investment, insurance), Special Account (retirement, investment), and MediSave Account (healthcare expenses), each serving different long-term purposes.

Should I verify this estimate against current official CPF contribution rates?

Yes, CPF contribution rates and allocation percentages can be adjusted by the Singapore government, so verify against current official CPF Board guidance before relying on this estimate.
