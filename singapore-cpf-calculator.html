Singapore CPF Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

The Central Provident Fund (CPF) is Singapore's mandatory social security savings scheme, requiring contributions from both employer and employee based on a percentage of wages, with the funds allocated across accounts for retirement, healthcare (Medisave), and housing. Contribution rates vary by the employee's age band and wage level, and are subject to a monthly wage ceiling. Because CPF rates and ceilings are periodically revised, this calculator keeps the rate editable so your estimate can reflect the current applicable percentage for your age group.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do CPF contribution rates vary by age?

Yes, CPF contribution rates for both employer and employee generally step down as employees get older, particularly after age 55 and beyond, reflecting different total contribution rate bands set by CPF Board.

Is there a wage ceiling for CPF contributions?

Yes, CPF contributions are typically capped based on a monthly wage ceiling and an annual additional wage ceiling — check CPF Board's current published figures.

What are the three main CPF accounts?

Contributions are generally allocated across the Ordinary Account (housing, education, investment), Special Account (retirement, investment), and Medisave Account (healthcare expenses).

Do permanent residents contribute to CPF at the same rate as citizens?

New permanent residents typically start at graduated contribution rates that increase over their first two to three years before reaching standard rates — check CPF Board guidance for specifics.

Where can I confirm current CPF contribution rates?

The CPF Board publishes current contribution rate tables by age band on its official website, which should be your reference for exact figures.
