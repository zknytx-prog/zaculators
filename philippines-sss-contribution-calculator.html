What This Calculator Estimates

Frequently Asked Questions

How are SSS contributions structured between employee and employer?

SSS contributions are split between employee and employer, with employers generally contributing a larger share, calculated based on which salary bracket the employee's monthly compensation falls into.

What benefits do SSS contributions eventually fund?

SSS contributions fund various benefits including retirement pensions, disability benefits, sickness benefits, maternity benefits, and death/funeral benefits for covered members and their beneficiaries.

How often are SSS contribution brackets and rates updated?

The SSS periodically reviews and can adjust contribution rates and salary brackets through official issuances, so using current official figures is important for an accurate contribution estimate.

Do self-employed individuals contribute differently to SSS?

Yes, self-employed and voluntary members typically contribute the full rate themselves (since there's no separate employer), differing from the employer-employee split structure used for regular employees.

Should I verify this estimate against current official SSS contribution tables?

Yes, contribution rates and salary brackets can be adjusted, so verify against the current official SSS contribution table before relying on this estimate for payroll or personal planning.
