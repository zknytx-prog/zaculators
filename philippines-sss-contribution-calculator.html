Philippines SSS Contribution Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

The Social Security System (SSS) in the Philippines requires mandatory contributions from employers and employees based on a monthly salary credit bracket system, funding benefits such as retirement pensions, disability, sickness, maternity, and death benefits. Because SSS uses bracket-based contribution tables that are periodically revised rather than one continuous flat rate, this calculator uses an editable rate to estimate contributions — check SSS's current official contribution table for your exact salary bracket.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SSS contribution a flat percentage or bracket-based?

SSS uses a bracket-based system where contribution amounts are tied to salary credit ranges rather than a single continuous percentage, so the effective rate can vary slightly across brackets.

Do both employer and employee contribute to SSS?

Yes, both employer and employee contribute, with the employer typically covering a larger share of the total contribution.

What benefits does SSS contribution fund?

SSS contributions fund benefits including retirement pensions, disability, sickness, maternity, and death/funeral benefits for members and qualified dependents.

Are self-employed individuals required to contribute to SSS?

Yes, self-employed and voluntary members can and often must contribute to SSS, typically at rates based on their declared monthly earnings.

Where can I check the current official SSS contribution table?

The Social Security System (SSS) publishes updated contribution tables on its official website and through official circulars — that's the best source for exact current figures.
