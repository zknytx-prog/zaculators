Frequently Asked Questions

How are EPF contributions typically split between employee and employer?

Malaysia's EPF system requires both employee and employer contributions, calculated as separate percentages of monthly wages, with the combined contribution building the employee's retirement savings over their working years.

Do EPF contribution rates vary by age?

Yes, Malaysian EPF contribution rates can differ for older employees (often those above a certain age threshold), reflecting adjusted rates as workers approach or pass typical retirement age.

Does EPF contribution rate depend on citizenship or residency status?

Yes, contribution rate structures can differ between Malaysian citizens/permanent residents and foreign workers, so confirm which rate schedule applies to your specific employment status.

What wages are included in the EPF contribution base?

Generally basic wages and most cash allowances form the contribution base, though certain payment types may be excluded — check current KWSP (EPF authority) guidance for specifics.

Should I verify this estimate against official current EPF rates?

Yes, contribution rates and thresholds can be adjusted by the Malaysian government, so verify against current official KWSP guidance before relying on this estimate for retirement planning.
