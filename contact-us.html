Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of inquiries can I send through this contact page?

Common inquiries include reporting a calculation error, suggesting a new calculator, general feedback about the site, or partnership and advertising inquiries.

How long does it typically take to get a response?

Response times can vary based on inquiry volume and complexity, though we aim to acknowledge and address genuine inquiries in a reasonable timeframe.

Can I request a new calculator to be added to the site?

Yes, calculator suggestions are welcome — while we can't guarantee every suggestion will be built, feedback helps inform what tools might be added in the future.

What should I do if I find an error in a calculation?

Please reach out with specific details about which calculator, what inputs you used, and what result seemed incorrect, so the issue can be properly investigated and corrected.

Do you offer support for using specific calculators?

General guidance is available for using the site's tools, though for complex financial, tax, legal, or medical decisions, we always recommend consulting an appropriate licensed professional.

Can I request a new calculator? ▼

Absolutely! We're always looking for new calculator ideas. Use the contact form and select "Calculator Suggestion" as the message type. Tell us what you need and why it would be useful.

Can I use your calculators on my website? ▼

Yes! Our calculators are available for embedding. Email us at hello@zaculators.com for partnership and embedding options.

Do you accept donations or sponsorships? ▼

We're currently supported by AdSense. For sponsorship or partnership inquiries, email hello@zaculators.com.

When will you respond to my message? ▼

We aim to respond within 24 hours during business days. During weekends and holidays, it may take 48-72 hours. Thanks for your patience!

How can I stay updated? ▼

Check back regularly for new calculators and features. We don't have a newsletter yet, but if you'd like one, let us know in the contact form!
