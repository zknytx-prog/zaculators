Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my province matter for salary calculations in Canada?

Each province and territory sets its own income tax brackets and rates on top of federal tax, so take-home pay for the same gross salary can differ meaningfully depending on where you live.

What are CPP and EI?

CPP (Canada Pension Plan) funds retirement benefits and EI (Employment Insurance) funds unemployment benefits — both are mandatory payroll deductions with their own contribution rates and annual maximums.

Does this calculator compute exact federal and provincial tax automatically?

No, pair this with the Federal Income Tax Calculator and check current provincial tax tables for a precise combined estimate.

Is there a maximum amount of CPP or EI I'll pay each year?

Yes, both CPP and EI have annual maximum contribution amounts based on a maximum insurable/pensionable earnings threshold set each year by the CRA and Service Canada.

How can I estimate my true take-home pay in Canada?

Combine this salary calculator with federal and provincial tax calculators, plus CPP and EI deductions, for the most complete estimate of net pay.
