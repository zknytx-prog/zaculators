Frequently Asked Questions

How does Uganda's PAYE tax band system work?

Salary income is divided into progressive bands, with each band taxed at its own specific rate — this graduated approach means higher earners pay progressively higher rates on the portion of income within higher bands.

What counts as taxable income under Uganda's PAYE system?

Taxable income generally includes basic salary and most cash allowances or benefits, though specific treatment can vary by benefit type — check current Uganda Revenue Authority guidance for details relevant to your situation.

How often does Uganda update its PAYE bands?

PAYE tax bands and rates can be revised periodically through government fiscal policy changes and annual budget announcements, so using current official figures ensures an accurate estimate.

Are there tax reliefs available under Uganda's PAYE system?

Certain reliefs or exemptions may apply depending on specific personal circumstances under Ugandan tax law — confirm current eligibility and amounts with official Uganda Revenue Authority guidance.

Should employers rely solely on this calculator for actual payroll compliance?

This provides a general estimate only — employers should use current official Uganda Revenue Authority PAYE tables and guidance for actual payroll processing and compliance.
