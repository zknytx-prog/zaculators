HRA Calculator India

What This Calculator Estimates

House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption in India allows salaried employees who pay rent to reduce their taxable income, with the exempt amount calculated as the lowest of three figures: actual HRA received, rent paid minus 10% of basic salary, or a percentage of basic salary that depends on whether you live in a metro or non-metro city. Because the calculation depends on several personal factors and city classification, this calculator provides a general estimate — confirm your exact exemption with a tax advisor or the Income Tax Department's guidance.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Gather your actual HRA received, rent paid, and basic salary figures before estimating — all three are needed for an accurate result.
  • Confirm whether your city qualifies as a metro for the higher exemption percentage.
  • Keep rent receipts and, if required, your landlord's PAN details ready for employer documentation.
  • Remember HRA exemption only applies if you actually pay rent — it doesn't apply to homeowners without rental payments.
  • Submit Form 12BB to your employer to have the exemption reflected in your payroll TDS calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What three figures determine HRA exemption?

The exemption is the lowest of: actual HRA received from your employer, rent paid minus 10% of basic salary, or 50% of basic salary (metro cities) / 40% (non-metro cities).

Which cities count as 'metro' for the higher HRA exemption rate?

Typically Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, and Chennai are classified as metro cities for this purpose, with all other cities using the lower percentage — confirm current classifications with official guidance.

Do I need to submit rent receipts to claim HRA exemption?

Yes, employers generally require rent receipts and, for larger amounts, your landlord's PAN details to process the exemption through payroll.

Can I claim HRA exemption if I don't pay rent?

No, HRA exemption requires actual rent payment. If you own your home and don't pay rent, you generally cannot claim this exemption.

Does this calculator replace the need for Form 12BB?

No, Form 12BB is the official declaration submitted to your employer for HRA exemption processing. This calculator is only for estimating the exemption amount.
