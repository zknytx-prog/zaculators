Frequently Asked Questions

How is end-of-service gratuity typically structured in Bahrain?

Bahraini labor law commonly structures gratuity at a lower rate for the first three years of service and a higher rate for years beyond that, calculated based on the employee's final basic salary rather than total compensation including allowances.

Does gratuity in Bahrain depend on how employment ends?

Yes, circumstances of termination (resignation versus dismissal, and reasons for either) can affect gratuity entitlement under Bahraini labor regulations, so reviewing your specific contract and applicable law is important.

Is basic salary or total salary used for gratuity calculations?

Typically basic salary (excluding allowances like housing or transport) forms the base for gratuity calculations, though this can vary by employment contract terms, so check your specific agreement.

Should expatriate and Bahraini national employees expect the same gratuity treatment?

End-of-service benefit structures in Bahrain generally apply based on labor law categories that can differ between social insurance-covered nationals and expatriate workers, so confirming which framework applies to your situation matters.

Should I verify this estimate against my actual employment contract?

Yes, this provides a general estimate based on common structures — always verify against your specific employment contract terms and current Bahraini labor law before relying on the figure for financial planning.
