VAT Calculator Saudi Arabia

What This Calculator Estimates

Value Added Tax in Saudi Arabia is administered by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) and applied at a flat statutory rate on most goods and services, with certain sectors zero-rated or exempt. Because VAT is a single percentage rather than a bracketed scale, this calculator multiplies your entered amount by the rate you provide — keep it current with ZATCA's published rate, since Saudi Arabia has adjusted its VAT rate before.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current VAT rate in Saudi Arabia?

ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority) sets and publishes Saudi Arabia's current VAT rate — confirm the current figure before relying on it for invoicing or filing, since it has changed before.

Are all goods and services subject to VAT in Saudi Arabia?

No, certain categories such as specific financial services and qualifying real estate transactions may be zero-rated or exempt — check ZATCA guidance for the current list.

Who is required to register for VAT in Saudi Arabia?

Businesses exceeding ZATCA's mandatory registration threshold in taxable supplies must register; voluntary registration is available below that threshold.

How is VAT-inclusive price calculated?

This calculator multiplies your amount by the VAT rate to estimate the tax, then adds that to your original amount for the VAT-inclusive total.

Does Zakat interact with VAT for businesses?

Zakat and VAT are separate obligations administered by the same authority (ZATCA) but calculated independently — this calculator addresses VAT only.
