PAYE Calculator Kenya

What This Calculator Estimates

Kenya's Pay As You Earn (PAYE) system is administered by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) and applies to employment income on a progressive band structure, meaning higher portions of income are taxed at higher rates rather than one flat rate across the whole salary. Employers withhold PAYE monthly and remit it to KRA, and employees can also claim personal relief and other statutory reliefs that reduce the final tax owed. Because bands and reliefs are reviewed periodically, this calculator uses an editable rate so you can plug in the current KRA rate for your income bracket and get an instant monthly and annual estimate rather than relying on a number that could be outdated.

Tips for a More Accurate Estimate

  • Keep your KRA PIN and payslip handy so you can cross-check this estimate against your actual payroll deduction.
  • If your employer provides benefits in kind (housing, car, etc.), remember those can affect your taxable income beyond base salary.
  • Personal relief reduces the tax you actually owe — factor it in separately since this tool estimates PAYE before relief.
  • Re-run the estimate whenever KRA publishes new bands, typically following changes in the Finance Act.
  • For exact figures needed for filing or disputes, always confirm with KRA's official tools or a licensed tax professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does this calculator use one editable rate instead of KRA's tax bands?

KRA applies several progressive bands rather than a single rate, and those bands are adjusted from time to time. Rather than hardcode figures that can go stale, this tool lets you enter the effective rate that applies to your bracket so the estimate stays accurate as rules change.

Does this include NHIF, NSSF, or housing levy deductions?

No. This estimates PAYE only. Kenyan payslips typically include several other statutory deductions on top of PAYE, so your net pay will differ from the PAYE figure alone.

Who is responsible for remitting PAYE in Kenya?

Employers withhold PAYE from employee salaries each month and remit it to KRA on the employee's behalf, though the tax liability itself belongs to the employee.

Is personal relief factored into this estimate?

Not automatically. If you want to account for personal relief, subtract it manually from the estimated PAYE figure or adjust the rate you enter.

Where can I confirm the current official PAYE bands?

The Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) publishes current PAYE bands and reliefs on its official website, and that should be your source for exact figures before filing.

This calculator provides PAYE estimates only. Official payroll rules and rates may vary, so verify current guidance before relying on the result.
