Income Tax Calculator India

What This Calculator Estimates

Indian income tax, administered by the Income Tax Department, offers taxpayers a choice between the old tax regime (with various exemptions and deductions like HRA and Section 80C investments) and the new tax regime (generally lower rates but with fewer deductions), both using progressive slabs. Because your effective tax burden depends heavily on which regime you choose and what deductions you claim, this calculator uses an editable rate so you can model either scenario based on your specific circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the old and new tax regimes in India?

The old regime allows various exemptions and deductions (like HRA, Section 80C investments, and home loan interest) but has higher slab rates, while the new regime offers lower slab rates but eliminates most deductions — you can generally choose whichever benefits you more.

Can I switch between tax regimes each year?

Salaried individuals can generally choose their preferred regime each financial year when filing, though there are some restrictions for those with business income — check current Income Tax Department rules.

Does this calculator apply deductions automatically?

No, factor in your applicable deductions (if using the old regime) before entering your taxable income, or adjust the rate to reflect your effective burden.

How often are India's tax slabs revised?

Slabs are typically revised through the annual Union Budget, so check for updates each fiscal year.

Where can I confirm current official tax slabs?

The Income Tax Department of India publishes current slab tables for both regimes on its official website (incometax.gov.in).

This calculator provides India income tax estimates only. It does not claim official tax results, and current rules, deductions, and filing choices may change the outcome.
