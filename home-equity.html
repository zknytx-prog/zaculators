What This Calculator Estimates

Home equity is the difference between your home's current market value and the outstanding balance on your mortgage, representing the portion of your home you actually own outright, which grows over time both through mortgage paydown and property value appreciation. This calculator estimates your current equity using the home value and remaining loan balance you provide, useful for understanding options like home equity loans or lines of credit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is home equity calculated?

Home equity is your home's current market value minus your outstanding mortgage balance — as you pay down the loan and/or the home's value increases, your equity grows.

Can home equity decrease?

Yes, if your home's market value declines significantly or you take on additional debt secured against the home (like a home equity loan), your equity can decrease even if you continue making mortgage payments.

What can I do with home equity?

Common options include a home equity loan (lump sum) or home equity line of credit (HELOC, flexible draw), often used for renovations, debt consolidation, or other major expenses — each has different terms and risks.

Is there a limit to how much equity I can borrow against?

Lenders typically limit borrowing to a percentage of your home's value minus your existing mortgage balance, so you generally can't borrow against 100% of your equity.

Does home value used in this calculation need to be exact?

For planning purposes, a recent appraisal or reasonable market estimate works well, though actual lending decisions typically require a formal appraisal for accuracy.
