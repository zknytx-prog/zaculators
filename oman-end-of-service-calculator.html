Oman End of Service Calculator

What This Calculator Estimates

End-of-service gratuity in Oman is a statutory benefit under Omani Labour Law for expatriate employees, generally calculated based on basic salary and years of continuous service, with formulas that can differ for earlier versus later years of employment. Omani nationals are typically covered under the Public Authority for Social Insurance (PASI) rather than end-of-service gratuity. This calculator gives a general planning estimate — confirm exact entitlements against your contract and current Labour Law provisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does end-of-service gratuity apply to Omani nationals?

Typically, Omani nationals are covered under the social insurance system (PASI) rather than the end-of-service gratuity scheme, which mainly applies to expatriate employees.

What salary figure is used for the calculation?

Generally the basic salary is used, excluding allowances, though this can depend on specific contract terms.

Is there a minimum service period required?

Typically at least one year of continuous service is required to qualify for any gratuity entitlement under Omani Labour Law.

Does the formula change for longer service periods?

Yes, Omani Labour Law has historically applied different rates for earlier years of service versus service beyond a certain threshold — verify current figures with official sources.

Can employment termination reasons affect the payout?

Certain circumstances, such as termination for cause, may affect gratuity entitlement under Omani law — consult a legal advisor for specifics of your situation.
