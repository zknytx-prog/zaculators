Frequently Asked Questions

How is end-of-service gratuity typically calculated in Oman?

Omani labor law commonly applies a graduated formula, with different gratuity rates for initial years of service compared to later years, calculated based on the employee's final basic salary.

Does gratuity apply differently to Omani nationals versus expatriates?

Yes, Omani nationals are generally covered under a social security/pension system, while end-of-service gratuity provisions under labor law typically apply specifically to expatriate workers — confirm which framework applies to your situation.

Is basic salary or total compensation used for the gratuity calculation?

Typically basic salary, excluding certain allowances, forms the calculation base under Omani labor regulations, though specific contract terms can vary, so check your employment agreement.

Does the reason for employment ending affect gratuity entitlement in Oman?

Yes, circumstances such as resignation versus termination, and specific contract terms, can affect gratuity calculation and entitlement under Omani labor law.

Should I verify this estimate against current Omani labor law?

Yes, always verify this general estimate against your specific employment contract terms and current Omani labor law before relying on the figure for financial planning.
