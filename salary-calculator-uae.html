Frequently Asked Questions

Does the UAE tax personal salary income?

No, the UAE does not levy personal income tax on salaries for residents, which is one of the key differences from many other countries' salary calculators.

What deductions might still apply to a UAE salary?

UAE nationals may have mandatory GOSI pension contributions, while expatriates typically have no mandatory salary deductions beyond company-specific policies, though end-of-service gratuity accrues separately.

Why are UAE salaries often split into basic and allowances?

Compensation structured with basic salary plus housing, transport, and other allowances affects end-of-service gratuity calculations (based on basic salary) and can affect certain benefit eligibility, which is why the breakdown matters.

Is this calculator useful for comparing job offers?

Yes, since there's no income tax to account for, comparing gross salary figures directly is more straightforward in the UAE than in countries with progressive income tax.

Does this factor in housing costs or cost of living?

No, this estimates salary figures only. Use a separate cost-of-living tool if you want to account for actual living expenses.

This calculator provides salary estimates only. Payroll, social contribution, and employer rules may vary, so verify official guidance before relying on the result.
