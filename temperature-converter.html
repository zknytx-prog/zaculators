Frequently Asked Questions

What's the formula for converting Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 and add 32 — for example, 20°C converts to (20 × 9/5) + 32 = 68°F.

What's the formula for converting Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit value, then multiply by 5/9 — for example, 68°F converts to (68 − 32) × 5/9 = 20°C.

How does Kelvin relate to Celsius?

Kelvin uses the same increment size as Celsius but starts at absolute zero — to convert Celsius to Kelvin, simply add 273.15.

Why do different countries use different temperature scales?

Historical and regional conventions vary — Fahrenheit remains common in the United States for everyday use, while Celsius is standard in most other countries, and Kelvin is used primarily in scientific contexts.

Is there a temperature where Celsius and Fahrenheit are equal?

Yes, -40 degrees is the same value on both the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales, a notable point where the two conversion formulas intersect.
